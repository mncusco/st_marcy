Journey Two · 21 Days

The Amazon Field Immersion

Not a tour. A structured, deeply guided field immersion in the Peruvian Amazon, for those willing to undertake the preparation it requires.

The Premise

Twenty-one days. Six participants. A single, sustained field immersion.

From Pucallpa we transfer by river into a remote basin region, where a curated lodge and village environment becomes the base for a sustained cycle of guided traditional practice and integration.

Selection is strict. Preparation is mandatory. The journey is not available without both.

The Arc

Five movements.

  1. 01

    Arrival · Pucallpa

    Two days of decompression at a curated hotel. Briefing, medical checks, equipment review.

  2. 02

    River Transfer

    Private boat transfer into a remote region of the basin. The transit itself is part of the immersion.

  3. 03

    Field Base

    Remote lodge and village setting. Living rhythm slows. Daylight, river, fire.

  4. 04

    Immersion Cycles

    Structured cycles of guided traditional practice alternating with full integration days.

  5. 05

    Return

    Reverse river transit, integration day in Pucallpa, departure.

The Immersion Core

Held within a local guidance system.

All practices are held by trusted local elders in their own setting. Alternation between cycle and integration days is non-negotiable — it is the structure that makes the depth possible.

  • AyahuasCa Cycles

    Structured, guided ceremonies in protected conditions with trusted local elders.

  • Cambo

    A traditional preparation administered within the local guidance system.

  • Bobinsana

    A plant practice for emotional integration, held within the cycle structure.

  • Rapé Integration

    Held in short integration sessions between cycles.

  • Additional Practices

    Further traditional plant-based practices may be offered as part of the local guidance system.

The Three-Month Protocol

Preparation is non-negotiable.

A three-month preparation protocol precedes the journey. It exists for safety, for focus, and to make integration possible. Without it, the journey is not offered.

  • Alcohol

    None for the full three months prior.

  • Recreational substances

    None for the full three months prior.

  • Diet

    Disciplined intake — full guidelines provided after selection.

  • Mental & emotional

    Daily preparation practice, journaling, reduced stimulation.

  • Sexual abstinence

    Recommended in the final preparation window.

  • Private coaching

    Mandatory one-to-one session with the guide before departure.
