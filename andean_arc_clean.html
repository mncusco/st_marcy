Journey One · 21 Days

The Andean Arc

A private cultural expedition from the Sacred Valley through the high Andes, the Amazon and the Pacific coast — twenty-one days, six participants, one curated path.

The Arc

Five chapters. Twenty-one days. Six participants. One curated path.

The Andean Arc is not a circuit of sights. It is a sequenced journey designed for depth — beginning in the Sacred Valley, rising into the high Andes, descending into the Amazon and closing on the Pacific coast.

Ritual is woven into the route, not sold as an addition. Hospitality is selected from boutique and heritage properties chosen for atmosphere, not for brand recognition.

The Route

Five chapters.

Chapter I · Days 1–4

Arrival into the Sacred Valley

  • Cusco

    Arrival, private welcome, acclimatisation. Restored colonial residence in the historic quarter.

  • Pisac

    Ancient market and Inca citadel. Ofrenda alla Pachamama — a quiet offering to the land before the journey begins.

  • Ollantaytambo

    One of the most intact Inca urban centres. Private guided interpretation at dawn.

  • Machu Picchu

    Arrival via the Belmond Hiram Bingham train. Early-access entry — the first to enter, before the valley opens to the wider world.

Chapter II · Days 5–8

Inner Journey

  • Preparation

    Preparatory fasting and setting of intention. Private briefing with the practitioner.

  • Private Ayahuasca Ceremony

    Held in a private maloca with a trusted local practitioner. Structured, protected, unhurried.

  • Kinsa Cocha

    Three sacred lagoons at altitude — a day of integration and silence in the high landscape.

  • Wachuma / San Pedro

    A daytime ceremony with a Lesley Myburg. The medicine of the Andes, in its native terrain.

Chapter III · Days 9–11

The High Andes

  • Humantay Lake

    Pre-dawn departure. A glacial lake at 4,200m — turquoise, improbable, silent. Private guides, unhurried pace.

  • Palcoyo

    The less-trafficked rainbow mountain — chosen for solitude over spectacle. Striped high-altitude ridgeline with unobstructed horizons.

  • Mountain Lodge

    Nights in lodge properties immersed in the Andean landscape. Rest, altitude, open sky.

Chapter IV · Days 12–15

Lake & Volcanoes

  • Lake Titicaca

    Premium lakeside lodge. Private vessel on the highest navigable lake in the world. Time with traditional island communities.

  • Colca Canyon

    One of the deepest canyons on Earth. Dawn observation of Andean condors in thermal flight.

  • Arequipa

    The white colonial city against El Misti volcano. Boutique heritage residence, private gastronomic evening.

Chapter V · Days 16–21

Desert & Pacific

  • Nazca

    Early-morning private flight over the Nazca Lines — geometries drawn by hands no one remembers, across a desert etched with mystery.

  • Ica

    Boutique desert hacienda. Private Pisco tasting in a working bodega. Dunes at golden hour.

  • Paracas

    Pacific desert reserve. Ballestas Islands wildlife. Paragliding. The ocean after three weeks in the mountains.

  • Pisco

    Private Tasting

  • Lima

    Closing dinner in Lima - Barranco — gastronomic curation. Private transfer and departure.

Ritual Integration

Embedded, not advertised.

Three ritual moments are woven into the journey itself. They are guided by Marcello with trusted local practitioners, held in safe and prepared environments, and are part of the sequence itself.

  • Pachamama Offering

    An arrival ceremony — a quiet offering to the land before the journey begins.

  • Ayahuasca Ceremony

    Held in La Pacha Temple under the guidance of Master Nacho Rodriguez and it's crew will be done in a structured and protected setting.

  • Wachuma (San Pedro)

    A daytime ceremony with a local facilitator, in a safe and prepared environment.
