I remember the first time I arrived at San Alejandro. The air was thick with the smell of wet earth and medicinal plants. Maestro Chichi was waiting for me under the palm roof of the maloca, with a quiet smile that said more than any word ever could.

That encounter changed something fundamental in me. Not because of a single ceremony or a dramatic vision — but because of the silence that followed. The space that opened inside me, slowly, over days of sitting with the forest.

I wrote this book to offer a glimpse into that experience. It is not a manual or a promise of transformation. It is simply a story — my story — of what it means to undertake a Master Plant Dieta in the heart of the Amazon, guided by someone who carries the medicine with genuine humility.

If you have ever felt called to the forest, I believe these pages will speak to you.
